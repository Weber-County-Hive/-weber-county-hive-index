How we handle tips

  • Private tips (Messenger, email, the tip form): the sender's name is never published, on this page or anywhere else.
  • Public comments and posts (Facebook and similar): shown with first and last initials only, when a name is available.
  • Your information stays yours. We don't use personal information from a tip unless you tell us we may.
  • Every tip is checked against public records: filings, bills, budgets, minutes and official notices.
  • We publish what the records confirm, label what they don't, and say when something is still being checked.
  • A tip is not a finding. Nothing goes into a case file until a record backs it.
