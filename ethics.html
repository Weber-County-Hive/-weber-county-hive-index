Our Code of Ethics

The Weber County Hive reports on Utah government, elections, development and campaign money using public records. These are the standards we hold ourselves to on every article, case page and post.

← Back to the Master Index
Posted Nov. 24, 2026 · Updated Nov. 24, 2026 · Call number HL 000.02

1. Public records first

  • Every finding comes from a public record: meeting minutes, campaign finance filings, conflict of interest forms, deeds, court records, state business filings and records we get through GRAMA.
  • We name the record next to each claim and link to it, so readers can check it for themselves.
  • We check every quote, date and figure against the original record before it is published.

2. Accuracy and corrections

  • If we get something wrong, we correct it publicly and say what changed.
  • Published figures show the date they were current. When new reports come out, we add them as new information instead of quietly changing what we published before.

3. Fair and careful language

  • We report what the records show. We don't decide guilt. That is for courts and the agencies responsible.
  • We use neutral words like “reported,” “disclosed” and “voted,” not words that state a legal conclusion.
  • When a process followed the required disclosure, notice and hearing rules, we say so, not only when it didn't.
  • We judge each vote, deal or filing on its own record (transparency, financial accountability and impact on the community), not on a person's reputation.

4. Independence

  • We never take money to report a story, leave one out, or change what we publish.
  • Everything we publish is free. Reader payments are optional donations that help cover the cost of records. A donation, from anyone, has no say in what we report.
  • The Hive does not endorse candidates, and our publisher's personal opinions stay out of what we publish.
  • Our publisher is not a lobbyist and takes no money for her opinions. She does not hold a government job and does not do paid consulting or public relations work about the Legislature or anything it is considering.

5. Disclosing our own connections

  • When our publisher has a personal or past work connection to a subject, we say so on the page.

6. Tips

  • We check every tip against public records before we publish anything from it. A tip is never published as fact on its own.
  • We keep a public log of tips and comments we checked, and what the records showed.

7. Conversations stay on the record

  • If someone we report on wants to talk about our reporting, the conversation stays public. We don't take comments off the record or have private conversations about our reporting. That way, nothing can be misunderstood later by either side.

8. Questions or corrections

  • Email webercountyhive@gmail.com. If you send a record that shows we got something wrong, we will check it and correct it publicly.
